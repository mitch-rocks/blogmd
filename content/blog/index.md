## Current Projects
### Engine Management System (Theta3)
This project has been a while in the making, it's for my company [Neutron Australia](https://neutron.au/). It's an insanely large project, especially just for a single developer who hasn't even graduated university yet. 

The purpose of an engine management system (ECU) is to read the value of various engine sensors, perform a bunch of math both on-demand and in the background, and schedule the triggering of hardware to precision of one microsecond (1us).

On paper it sounds simple, but it involves the design and development of hardware, the firmware running on the ECU itself, and the tuning software. All three of these are relatively tightly coupled to eachother, mostly the firmware and tuning software, and I am actively implementing mechanisms that make `ECU <--> Tuning Software` communication much more dynamic.
